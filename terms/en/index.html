What Elio does

Elio helps you note your baby's feeds, sleep, diapers and growth, keep track of how you are doing, and see patterns in what you have recorded. It is a personal tracking tool.

What Elio is not

It is not a medical device and it replaces neither a doctor, nor a midwife, nor an emergency service. The assistant answers from your own entries and general knowledge: it does not diagnose. When something about your baby's health worries you, call a professional. For a fever before 3 months, laboured breathing, or a very floppy baby, call your emergency number (15 or 112 in France).

Your account

The app works with no account. If you create one to sync, you are responsible for keeping your credentials private. An invitation code shares the baby's tracking with the co-parent you link, never your personal diary.
